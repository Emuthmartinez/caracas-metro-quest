# Metro Quest: Caracas — Open Questions for GATE 0

Each question lists the gap or conflict, 2–3 options, and a recommendation. Per Hard
Rule 1 nothing below is decided until you pick; canon freezes with your answers.
Questions marked **⚠ CONFLICT** are places where the master build plan contradicts
shipped canon — these especially need your call.

---

## Q1. ⚠ CONFLICT — Starters

The build plan locks the starters as **chigüire (water), guacamaya (fire/flying),
oso frontino (grass)**. Canon already ships a different trio with a balanced
triangle: **Frontinito (Tepuy), Turpialín (Rumba/Monte), Cocuyín (Catatumbo)** — and
Chigüi and Guacamayo already exist as regular dex creatures with their own lore.

- **A (recommended).** Keep the canon trio. Chigüi and Guacamayo stay regular
  species and gain full three-stage lines in Phase 4. The plan's starter list is
  amended.
- **B.** Adopt the plan's trio: promote Chigüi and Guacamayo to starters with new
  three-stage lines, demote Turpialín/Cocuyín to regular lines. Breaks shipped canon
  and the existing intro scene.
- **C.** Canon trio stays for the main game; the plan's trio become "second
  starters" gifted at a later region (FireRed's Hoenn-starter pattern).

## Q2. ⚠ CONFLICT — Type system: 8 vs 12–16

Canon has 8 types with a shipped, balanced chart and a starter triangle. The plan
asks for 12–16 (suggesting Espíritu/Urbano/Selva/Subterráneo — which overlap
Espanto/Criollo/Monte/Tepuy almost 1:1).

- **A (recommended).** Keep the 8 canon types. They are distinctive, culturally
  rooted, and already balanced; 150 creatures ÷ 8 types ≈ 19 per type, plenty.
- **B.** Extend to 10–12 by *adding* types without renaming or re-routing existing
  matchups (candidates rooted in canon: **Llanero** (the plains), **Andino**
  (páramo/cold), **Urbano** split out of Criollo, **Encanto** for the
  encantado/water-spirit layer). Existing creatures keep their types.
- **C.** Full redesign to 16. Maximum FireRed parity, but invalidates the shipped
  chart, triangle, and all 29 typings.

## Q3. ⚠ CONFLICT — Stat model: 4 stats vs Gen-3 six

Canon ships HP/ATK/DEF/SPD and `DESIGN.md` explicitly rejects the physical/special
split ("✗ a propósito"). The plan demands FireRed depth: 6 stats, IVs, EVs, natures.

- **A.** Honor the canon note: stay 4-stat, adapt IV/EV/natures onto it. Simpler,
  but caps depth — many FireRed systems (Sp.Atk natures, special walls) lose meaning.
- **B (recommended).** Amend canon: move to 6 stats (HP/ATK/DEF/SPA/SPD/SPE) with
  the Gen-3 *by-type* physical/special split. The "a propósito" note read as a v1
  scoping choice, not a world rule — but it is a written canon note, so this needs
  your explicit amendment.
- **C.** Middle path: 5 stats (one Special covering both, like actual Gen 1).

## Q4. ⚠ CONFLICT — Roster: the 29 inside the 150

The plan wants 150 creatures (~58 lines). Canon's 29 exist with names, types, stats,
cries, and dex entries.

- **A (recommended).** The 29 are a frozen subset: names, types, evolution
  relationships, and lore hooks unchanged; stats may be rebalanced to the new
  budgets; some singles may *gain* stages (e.g., Cunaguaro, Tonina pre-evos) since
  adding is not contradicting.
- **B.** The 29 are fully frozen including stats and line shapes; the other 121
  creatures absorb all balance pressure.
- **C.** Treat the 29 as inspiration only and redesign freely. (Violates Hard Rule 1;
  listed for completeness.)

## Q5. ⚠ CONFLICT — Villain faction

Phase 7 asks for a "villain faction from lore." Canon's ending rule: **"El final no
tiene villano"** — the Tren Fantasma is a guardian, not an enemy.

- **A (recommended).** Keep the no-villain ending absolutely. Introduce a *human*
  antagonist faction for the middle acts that exploits the Hora Fantasma (e.g.,
  **los Bachaqueros de Recuerdos** — memory scalpers who bottle and resell what the
  Tren collects), whose arc resolves in understanding, not destruction. The Tren
  remains the non-villain finale.
- **B.** No faction at all: the antagonist is forgetting itself; conflict comes from
  Jefes, rival, and wild legendaries. Purest canon, thinnest plot for a 4×-larger game.
- **C.** A folkloric antagonist court (e.g., espantos who *want* the city forgotten
  so they can keep the tunnels). Riskier: edges folklore toward villainy, against
  Hard Rule 6.

## Q6. World scale and where the Tren Fantasma sits

Canon's story spans Línea 1 + Línea Fantasma, with the Tren as final boss.
The plan expands to the full network (Líneas 1–6, Metrocable, Los Teques, Ávila).

- **A (recommended).** Línea 1 = Act 1 (the shipped game becomes the opening
  region); Líneas 2/3/4 + surface zones = Acts 2–3; the Tren Fantasma encounter
  moves to the *end of Act 3* on the Línea Fantasma; Metrocable/Ávila summit/Los
  Teques = post-game (Sevii pattern). The 4 canon Jefes keep their stations; new
  Line Guardians are added at other terminals (see Q7).
- **B.** Keep the canon story exactly as is (Tren after 4 badges) and make the rest
  of the network 100% post-game. Least canon risk, but front-loads the ending.
- **C.** Network open from early; story gated by fichas/field moves rather than line
  order (more Crystal than FireRed).

## Q7. Badge count: 4 Fichas Doradas vs FireRed's 8

- **A (recommended).** Expand to **8 Fichas Doradas**: the 4 canon Jefes unchanged
  + 4 new Jefes at new-region terminals (e.g., El Silencio/La Rinconada/Zoológico/
  Palo Verde — proposed in Phase 2). The Elite-Four equivalent does not exist in
  canon: propose **el Consejo de la Hora Fantasma** at Zona Rental — four legendary
  ánimas heard before the Línea Fantasma opens.
- **B.** Keep 4 badges; make each a multi-stage trial. Shorter macro-structure than
  FireRed parity implies.
- **C.** 8 badges by *adding trials retroactively* to the 4 canon Jefes (each guards
  two). Strains the shipped scripts.

## Q8. Status conditions: 3 shipped vs the classic six

Canon ships ENV/PAR/DRM. FireRed has burn, freeze, plus volatile statuses
(confusion, infatuation…).

- **A (recommended).** Add lore-justified equivalents in Phase 1: **QUE** (Quemado —
  tercer riel/Centella), **confusion** as *mareado* (hora-pico crowd spin), and
  replace freeze with **PAV** (empavado — la pava: can't act until "knocked on
  wood"). All exact mechanics specced in Phase 1, your sign-off there.
- **B.** Keep the shipped three only. Simpler; weakens type/move variety at 250 moves.

## Q9. Day/night and the Hora Fantasma

Canon frames the whole game inside the Hora Fantasma (FireRed itself has no clock).

- **A (recommended).** No real clock; the Hora Fantasma is the eternal "night" of
  the underground, but **surface zones are golden-hour day** — the contrast is
  thematic, location-based, deterministic. Weather/field effects (apagón, lluvia,
  neblina del Ávila) come from map + story state.
- **B.** Real-time day/night that gates some espantos (la Sayona at night only).
  More sim, contradicts the "one long hora fantasma" frame.

## Q10. Sacred-figure rule scope

Canon: María Lionza and el Doctorcito bless/heal, never battle or get fichados.

- **A (recommended).** Generalize the rule: any figure of *living devotion* (María
  Lionza and her cortes, el Doctorcito/José Gregorio, ánimas veneradas like el Ánima
  Sola) is NPC-only — they may gate, bless, heal, or quest-give. Folkloric
  *espantos* (Silbón, Sayona, Llorona, duendes) remain fichables since canon already
  fiches them. The María Lionza tradition may inspire *original* creature designs
  only with your explicit per-creature approval.
- **B.** Stricter: nothing connected to the Sorte/María Lionza tradition appears as
  a creature at all, even originals.

## Q11. ⚠ CONFLICT — Art pipeline: procedural code-drawn pixels vs generated assets

The shipped game draws *everything* in code (16-column pixel grids in `js/dex.js`,
zero image files except the intro/keyart) — and "zero dependencies, opens from a
link" is a written design principle. The plan's Phases 3/5/6 build a
Higgsfield-generated PNG asset pipeline at FireRed fidelity (64×64 sprites, tilesets,
backgrounds).

- **A (recommended).** Amend canon: move to generated + post-processed PNG sprite
  sheets (the FireRed-quality mandate realistically requires it). "Zero
  dependencies" is preserved in spirit — still static files, no build, no CDN; the
  game just loads its own committed PNGs. The existing code-drawn art remains as
  v1 historical reference.
- **B.** Hybrid: generated art only for *new* asset classes (battle scenes 64×64,
  backgrounds, key art); the 16×16 overworld stays procedural. Cheaper, but two art
  systems forever.
- **C.** Stay fully procedural and use Higgsfield only for concept art the
  pixel-artist (me, in code) transcribes. Maximum canon fidelity, hardest to reach
  FireRed quality.

## Q12. Language of record

Canon: "Español venezolano sin subtítulos ni disculpas." The plan requires
Spanish-first content with full English localization.

- **A (recommended).** Spanish remains the language of record and default; English
  is a *localization* (Phase 7) that deliberately keeps chamo/arepa/station names
  untranslated per the future localization guide. The "sin subtítulos" line is read
  as tone (no apologizing/exoticizing), not a ban on an EN build.
- **B.** Spanish-only product; English limited to developer docs. Simplest, halves
  the audience the plan targets.

# Metro Quest: Caracas — World Bible (Phase 2, for GATE 2)

**Status:** Draft for GATE 2 review. No tile is drawn until this closes.
**Canon constraints honored:** the shipped Línea 1 route and its 9 station
identities are unchanged (Act 1); Línea 5 is la Línea Fantasma (canon); the
sacred-figure rule (lore bible §2.5) governs every NPC below; act structure per
GATE 0/Q6A; 8 Fichas per Q7A.
**Real-network sources:** Wikipedia's Caracas Metro station list (verified
2026-06-11), Metrocable San Agustín (5 stations), Metrocable Mariche (express),
Los Teques Metro (8 stations).
**Companions:** `data/maps/maps.schema.json`, `data/maps/maps.json` (generated by
`scripts/gen_maps.py`), both delivered with this phase.

---

## 0. The world in one paragraph

The game world **is** the real network. Five metro lines + two Metrocables + the
Los Teques line + the surface above them + el Ávila above everything. Each line is
a region with its own ecological and folkloric identity; each station is a town;
each inter-station tunnel is a route with its own encounter table; minor real
stations appear as **paradas menores** inside tunnel routes (small lit platforms:
save point, one or two NPCs, no full services) so the real network is complete
without bloating the canon map graph. Underground it is always la Hora Fantasma;
the surface is always golden hour (Q9A).

## 1. Region I — LÍNEA 1, «La Espina Dorsal» (Act 1)

The shipped canon region, now seated in the full 22-station real line.
**Identity:** the city's backbone; everyday Caracas; all eight types appear in
low-level forms. **Levels 3–30. Jefes 1–4 (canon).**

Major hubs (canon identities frozen): **Propatria** (first steps, first tunnel),
**Caño Amarillo** (gateway to El Calvario's monte — Machete country),
**Capitolio** (Doña Bárbara, Ficha del Centro; transfer walk to El Silencio,
locked until Act 2), **Bellas Artes** (el Olvidadizo; Parque Los Caobos surface
zone; los museos), **Plaza Venezuela** (el Rumbero Mayor, Ficha de la Fuente; the
16-million-color fountain; **the locked Línea 5 gate** — canon — and the L3
transfer, locked until Act 2 ends), **Sabana Grande** (boulevard surface zone,
chess players, la Patineta gift, first Bachaqueros de Recuerdos sighting),
**Chacaíto** (Valentina la Sifrina, Ficha del Este), **Altamira** (Puerta del
Ávila surface access; obelisk plaza), **Petare** (Doña Petra, Ficha del Pueblo;
mercado surface zone; Metrocable Mariche dock, locked until post-game).

Paradas menores (in their tunnel routes): Pérez Bonalde, Plaza Sucre, Gato Negro,
Agua Salud · La Hoyada, Parque Carabobo · Colegio de Ingenieros · Chacao ·
Miranda (Parque del Este surface zone), Los Dos Caminos, Los Cortijos, La
California · Palo Verde (east cap; Mariche dock vestibule).

**Story beats:** Abuela's send-off (canon) → Cheo 1 (Caño Amarillo, canon) →
Bachaqueros vendor on the boulevard bottling "recuerdos embotellados" → Cheo 2
(Altamira) → Ficha del Pueblo → the Bachaqueros' truck leaves for the west: Act 2.

## 2. Region II — LÍNEA 2 + RAMAL ZOOLÓGICO, «El Oeste Profundo» (Act 2)

**Identity:** the working-class west where the monte reclaims old industry; Monte
and Caribe country, Espanto in the dark segments. The line splits after
**Mamera**: one branch to **Caricuao → Zoológico**, the other to **Ruiz Pineda →
Las Adjuntas**. **Levels 30–38. Jefes 5–6.**

- **El Silencio** — twin towers and arcades; entry from the Capitolio transfer
  walk; the west's front door. Services full; Chismógrafo vendor.
- **Capuchinos** — the L4 junction, locked until Act 3; an old chapel above gives
  the parada its hush.
- **La Yaguara** — industrial sheds over the Guaire. **Jefe 6: el Pescador del
  Guaire (Caribe), Ficha del Río** — the man who fishes the unfishable river and
  returns what the city threw away. Bachaqueros' bottling plant side-dungeon.
- **Antímano / Carapita / Mamera** — barrio stations stacked on hillsides; the
  tunnel segments between them are **apagón zones (Luz de Cocuyo required)**.
- **Caricuao** — parkside family station, daycare: **la Tía que Cuida**.
- **Zoológico** — the Caricuao zoo as our **Safari Zone** (fee, Fichas de Feria,
  bait/distract, step limit; exotic Monte/Tepuy/Caribe tables). **Jefe 5: el
  Baquiano del Zoológico (Monte), Ficha del Monte** — the keeper who stayed when
  the visitors stopped coming, so the animals stayed too.
- **Ruiz Pineda / Las Adjuntas** — the quiet far west; Las Adjuntas is the sealed
  door to Los Teques (post-game) and where Cheo 3 happens: he's scouting the line
  that leaves the city, the way he once did.
- Paradas menores: Maternidad, Artigas, La Paz.

## 3. Region III — LÍNEA 3, «El Sur de los Caballos» (Acts 2–3)

**Identity:** markets, terminals, the hippodrome; Criollo and Sabroso heartland,
Tepuy toward the southern hills. Opens at Plaza Venezuela after Ficha del Monte.
**Levels 34–42. Jefe 7.**

- **Ciudad Universitaria** — the UCV, Patrimonio de la Humanidad (canon glossary);
  surface zone «Tierra de Nadie»; the Estudiante Eterno's rumors begin here.
- **Los Símbolos / La Bandera** — bus-terminal chaos, peak Criollo encounter
  rates; La Bandera's andén concurrido is the densest in the game.
- **El Valle / Los Jardines** — a flooded quebrada segment between them is the
  mandatory **Nado gate** (after Ficha del Río).
- **Coche / Mercado** — the wholesale market at dawn: Sabroso tables, vitamin
  vendors, the buhonero guild's headquarters.
- **La Rinconada** — the hippodrome. **Jefe 7: la Amazona de La Rinconada
  (Criollo), Ficha del Sur** — her horses run the hora fantasma against riders
  only she can see. Gateway to the southern surface zone (Valles del Tuy rail
  vestibule, sealed; flavor only).

## 4. Region IV — LÍNEA 4, «El Corazón Escondido» (Act 3)

**Identity:** theaters, the old bullring, the towers; Espanto/Rumba/Catatumbo at
high level; the city's cultural memory compressed into five stops. Opens via
Capuchinos after Ficha del Sur. **Levels 42–50. Jefe 8 + el Consejo.**

- **Teatros** — the Teatro Municipal's velvet dark; Rumba/Espanto encounters; la
  Coplera's sister teaches the starter ultimates' tutor moves here.
- **Nuevo Circo** — the empty bullring: the **Bachaqueros' black market** and the
  arc's resolution: their boss, **el Coleccionista**, is an emigrant who couldn't
  carry his own memories and began hoarding everyone else's. No villain to
  destroy (canon law): you battle his guard, then he opens his bottles himself.
  Demolición clears the rubble ring-gates.
- **Parque Central** — under the twin towers. **Jefe 8: la Ingeniera de las
  Torres (Catatumbo), Ficha de la Torre** — she has kept the towers lit through
  every apagón since before you were born. Metrocable San Agustín dock (Teleférico
  oficio taught here).
- **Zona Rental** — canon's threshold. **El Consejo de la Hora Fantasma**: four
  ánimas of the city's memory, fought back-to-back — **el Obrero del 83**
  (Tepuy/Criollo, he dug these tunnels), **la Maestra Eterna** (Criollo/Espanto,
  every chamo in Caracas was her student), **el Estudiante de la UCV**
  (Catatumbo/Rumba, forever one semester from graduating), **la Cantora de
  Velorios** (Rumba/Espanto, she sings the dead to sleep and the living to
  tears). Then **Cheo, the final battle** — and what he finally says.
- Parada menor: Capuchinos vestibule (junction).

## 5. Region V — LA LÍNEA FANTASMA (Línea 5) — finale

Canon region, now seated on the real L5 alignment: **Bello Monte** (the one real,
half-finished station — lights on, no trains) then the ghost stations **Las
Mercedes, Tamanaco, Chuao, Bello Campo, Miranda**, each a memory-tableau built
from what emigrants left behind (a kitchen, a classroom, a quinceañera hall, an
airport gate, an unfinished house). María Lionza grants the Ficha de Oro at the
gate (canon; sacred, NPC-only). At the line's end: **el Tren Fantasma, L60** —
resolved per canon: you help it deliver the memories *de ida y de vuelta*.
**Requires: 8 Fichas + Consejo cleared + la bendición.**

## 6. Surface zones & el Ávila

**El Calvario** (canon, Machete), **Bulevar de Sabana Grande** (chess, buskers,
Bachaqueros intro), **Parque Los Caobos** (Bellas Artes; sloth tree — Pereza,
canon), **Tierra de Nadie / UCV** (Ciudad Universitaria), **Parque del Este**
(Miranda parada; Tonina's pond rumor), **Mercado de Petare** (canon), **La
Hípica Sur** (La Rinconada). 

**El Ávila (Waraira Repano)** — the looming north (canon: sin Ávila no hay
norte). Four maps: **Puerta del Ávila** (Altamira, Act 1 teaser, Machete),
**Sabas Nieves** (Act 3, Tepuy/Monte highlands), **La Neblina** (permanent
neblina weather; La Llorona's ravines, canon), **La Cumbre / Hotel Humboldt**
(post-game; Teleférico required; **Catatumbo legendary, L70** — canon
post-final). Ucumarí guards the trails (canon: portero secreto del Ávila).

## 7. Vertical regions — los Metrocables (post-game, Teleférico oficio)

- **Metrocable San Agustín** (from Parque Central): Hornos de Cal → La Ceiba →
  El Manguito → San Agustín. Identity: **the cradle of salsa caraqueña** — Rumba
  region in the sky; rooftop encounter tables, the Rumbero Mayor's origin quest,
  a tambor school that re-teaches Rumba moves.
- **Metrocable Mariche** (express from Palo Verde): one long ride over the city's
  east edge to **Mariche** — the frontier where the urban-magical creatures thin
  out and the old monte begins; highest-level wild tables outside el Ávila.

## 8. Post-game — LÍNEA LOS TEQUES, «La Línea que se Fue» (the Sevii pattern)

Opens at Las Adjuntas after the Tren Fantasma is helped. Eight stations leaving
the valley: **Alí Primera → Guaicaipuro → Independencia → Los Cerritos →
Carrizal → Las Minas → San Antonio**. Identity: the commuter diaspora in
miniature — the people who left the city but not the country; Andean-flavored
fog, Tepuy/Espanto tables, **La Torre** battle facility at Independencia,
**el Carretón** roams the whole region (canon wishlist espanto), Cheo's weekly
rematch at San Antonio (he's thinking about staying).

## 11. Map data: schema & conventions

Schema: `data/maps/maps.schema.json`. Instance: `data/maps/maps.json` (generated
by `scripts/gen_maps.py` — edit the line definitions there, never the JSON by
hand). Conventions:
- **ids**: `st_<station>`, `tn_<a>__<b>` (tunnel), `sf_<zone>` (surface),
  `in_<place>` (interior), `cb_<line>_<leg>` (cable), `gh_<station>` (ghost).
- **type**: `station | tunnel | surface | interior | cable | ghost`.
- **tileset ref**: `ts.<region>` (Phase 3/6 produce them).
- **encounter ref**: `enc.<map_id>` — tables filled in Phase 4; empty = safe map.
- **music ref**: `mus.<region>.<context>` — Phase 8 fills the tracklist.
- **NPC/event lists**: seeded empty; Phase 7 populates; the canon Act 1 maps keep
  their shipped NPCs (migration mapping in the file's `canon_map` field).
- Paradas menores are `features` of their tunnel map, not standalone maps.
